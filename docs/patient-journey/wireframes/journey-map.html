Off the happy path

Emergency

Any screen. Site shows "Call 112" in the FAQ and care dialog. Keep it on 04 and 09 too.

Not available in my country / under 18

Caught at 04, before clinician choice or payment. Under-18s are booked by a guardian as a dependant.

Video fails

From 09 or 10: free rebook, back to 05 with the service and patient pre-filled.

Not suitable online

At 10 the clinician recommends in-person care. 11 shows a referral instead of a prescription.

What reading the code showed

  • The repo ends at screen 03. "Explore on Carevia", "Create your account" and "Visit Carevia" all link to the external site. Booking, payment, video and the account are not here.
  • The handoff loses context. The care dialog always links to …/#services, whichever service is open. A patient who picked Mental health lands on the generic list and picks again. A ?service=mental param would carry the choice to screen 05.
  • "Find your care" just scrolls. The nav button, the hero's "Browse our care" and the footer CTA all jump to #care on the same page. That's fine for discovery, but nothing on the site acts as a "Book now".
  • Eligibility is only in the FAQ. The 18+ rule and the country limit sit in a collapsed answer. Screen 04 brings them forward, before money is involved.
