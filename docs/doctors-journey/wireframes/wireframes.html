Section 1

The journey in four parts

A doctor touches Carevia in four parts. Onboarding (part 2) happens once; the working day (part 3) is where the product must stay out of the way.

1Apply and be verifiedJoin Carevia, apply in five minutes. Carevia checks the cédula with the Ordem, identity, specialty and insurance, then decides.D01–A02Careful, human-paced
2Get a login and set upAn admin creates the profile and sends an invite. The doctor sets a password, secures the account, checks the public profile, accepts how Carevia work works, opens hours, tests the camera.A03–D10Guided, resumable
3The working dayAgenda, prepare, call as room owner, one of four outcomes, write it up in iMED. Repeated daily.D11–D15Fast, quiet
4Run the practice, then leave wellHours, time off, history, account. Pausing or leaving never strands a booked patient.D16–A04Self-serve, reversible

A private space for every call. You consult from somewhere private and quiet, with usable light.
Patients see who you are. Your professional name and cédula show on your profile and in the visit.

Poor quality ends the visit. If sound or picture is too poor for a safe opinion, end it, record the Carevia outcome, and write the reason in iMED.
Not an emergency service. Patients are told to call 112 in an emergency. You see patients who are in mainland Portugal.

Section 6 · When things go wrong

E01–E04 · Exceptions, and the way out of each

New states
E01 · The patient never joinsIn D12–D14
Prepare shows “In the waiting room: no”. After the start time the doctor may wait, or end and choose Patient didn’t join. The visit closes with that outcome; the patient sees the call didn’t connect. No charge argument starts in Carevia: refund wording follows the cancellation terms.
E02 · The connection is too poorExists
Either side drops: the same appointment stays open inside the join window. The doctor can rejoin, or record Ended: poor connection. The patient rejoins free or picks a new time with the same visit, never a second payment. The clinical reason, if any, is written in iMED.
E03 · The doctor cannot do a visitNew
From the visit: I can’t do this visit. One confirmation names the result before it happens:
Cancel the 10:10 visit with Patient Name?
The patient is told the visit is cancelled, offered a new time with you or another doctor in General & urgent care, and the cancellation terms apply. The visit is kept as cancelled, never deleted. Cancel this visit Keep the visit
E04 · Paused, revoked, or not linkedNew
One plain page per state, and no agenda behind it: not linked (“Your login isn’t linked to a doctor profile yet”), paused (by you or the team, with the date it lifts), two-factor needed (a button into D19). Each says who to contact.
What never happens
A visit is deleted. Cancellations are states, with the patient told.
A second appointment is created to fix the first. Reschedule moves the same one.
A reason is typed into Carevia. Outcomes are four choices; reasons live in iMED.
A patient is stranded by a pause or close-down. Booked visits are dealt with first, one by one.
A04 · Close down a doctor (admin)
Refused while confirmed future visits exist, and lists them. When none remain: profile hidden, open times closed, login disabled (kept, so access logs still name their actor). The clinical record stays in iMED, with its depositary.
Pause this doctorClose down
Video is off (no Daily key)
The call step shows the existing “video is off” state for the doctor too: wait, or record the outcome. The agenda never pretends a call can start.
