# Sources for Portuguese telemedicine constraints

Research date: 4 November 2026. Re-read the primary documents before launch. Secondary clinic blogs are not authority.

## Regulator and professional rules

- ERS registration, including telemedicine units aimed at patients in mainland Portugal even when servers are abroad: [ERS FAQ on registration](https://ers.pt/pt/prestadores/perguntas-frequentes/faqs/perguntas-frequentes-sobre-obrigatoriedade-de-registo-na-ers/)
- ERS registration brochure (fixed, mobile, and telemedicine establishments): [ERS triptych](https://www.ers.pt/media/qkwl5hrc/triptico_registo-de-establecimentos_web.pdf)
- ERS Alerta de supervisão n.º 7/2024 (consent, environment, privacy, clinical record, attendance proof, accompaniment, response times): [Alerta 7/2024](https://www.ers.pt/pt/atividade/supervisao/selecionar/alertas-de-supervisao/2024/alertas/alerta-de-supervis%C3%A3o-n-%C2%BA-7-2024/)
- ERS patient FAQ. Teleconsulta includes video, telephone, email, or other digital means. Recording of sound or image needs written consent. SNS establishments need written consent for the teleconsulta itself: [Direitos dos utentes na prestação de teleconsultas](https://www.ers.pt/pt/utentes/perguntas-frequentes/faq/direitos-dos-utentes-na-prestacao-de-teleconsultas/)
- ERS practical guide: [Guia prático teleconsultas](https://www.ers.pt/media/5mmkeoix/guia-pratico-teleconsultas.pdf) and [study PDF](https://www.ers.pt/media/bmfey0rn/estudo-teleconsultas07-24.pdf)
- DGS Norma n.º 010/2015 (written consent and mandatory electronic teleconsulta fields; written for the SNS circuit): [Norma 010/2015](https://www.dgs.pt/directrizes-da-dgs/normas-e-circulares-normativas/norma-n-0102015-de-15062015-pdf.aspx)
- Ordem dos Médicos deontology for telemedicine, Código Deontológico arts. 46–49, and warning against prescriptions or certificates without a real assessment: [Parecer, OMSUL copy](https://www.omsul.pt/Portals/0/docs/Parecer-Exercicio-da-Telemedicina.pdf)

## Health information

- Lei n.º 12/2005, health information belongs to the person and providers are depositaries: [Diário da República](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/12-2005-624463)
- ERS FAQ on access to health information, also citing Lei n.º 95/2019: [Acesso à informação de saúde](https://ers.pt/pt/utentes/perguntas-frequentes/faq/direito-de-acesso-a-informacao-de-saude-e-a-protecao-de-dados-pessoais/)

## Prescriptions and CIT

- SPMS description of PEM, Lei n.º 11/2012 and Portaria n.º 137-A/2012: [PEM | SPMS](https://www.spms.min-saude.pt/2020/07/pem/)
- Technical rules for prescription software, version 4.1, May 2024, including online BDNP and RNU checks: [SPMS PDF](https://pem.spms.min-saude.pt/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Especificacoes_tecnicas-relativas-aos-softwares-de-prescricao-de-medicamentos-e-produtos-de-saude.V.4.10.pdf)
- Consolidated prescription and dispensing rules: [Portaria, Diário da República](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/portaria/2015-74448401)
- ACSS prescribing norms, electronic prescription required, including controlled medicines, through SPMS-certified software or the Ministry PEM app: [Normas de prescrição](https://www.acss.min-saude.pt/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Normas_Prescricao_20240304_vf.pdf)
- CIT extended to private and social providers registered with the ERS from 1 March 2024; electronic transmission for records in the SPMS central database: [SPMS certificados eletrónicos](https://www.spms.min-saude.pt/2015/10/ce-certificados-eletronicos/)

## Do not treat as authority

Clinic marketing pages that say a teleconsulta "can" issue a CIT or prescription. Those describe clinical possibility, not a right of the patient to receive the document.
