Please read this before your consultation. It explains how the visit works and what it can and can't do.

## How your consultation works

Your consultation is a live video call with a doctor, in your browser. Before the call, you see your doctor's professional name and their Ordem dos Médicos licence number (cédula).

Carevia is provided by [legal entity name]. Entidade Reguladora da Saúde (ERS) registration: [ERS registration number].

Before you join, you confirm that the email and mobile number for this visit are yours. This helps the doctor know they are speaking to the right person.

## When you need to be seen in person

An online consultation can't replace every visit. If your doctor needs to examine you, or your situation is urgent, they will tell you to be seen in person instead. You can also choose to see a doctor in person at any time.

If the sound or picture is not good enough for your doctor to assess you safely, they may end the call. You can reconnect while the visit is still open, or move the same appointment to a new time.

## Price

You see the full price of the consultation, including any other charges, before you pay. Your visit is only confirmed after payment.

## Recording

Sound and image are not recorded. Not by Carevia, and not by your doctor.

## Not for emergencies

Carevia is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. Don't wait for your consultation.

## What happens after

Your doctor decides, after assessing you, whether anything needs to be issued. Nothing is decided before the consultation, and booking a visit doesn't guarantee a particular outcome.

Your clinical record, and any document your doctor issues, is kept in iMED, Carevia's clinical system. Carevia doesn't create medical documents.

## Bringing someone with you

You can have someone with you during the consultation, such as a family member or carer, just as you could at an in-person visit. They can sit with you, or join from their own device with a separate link. Please tell the doctor at the start who is with you.

If the consultation is for your child, stay with them for the whole call.

## Find a private space

Choose a quiet, private place with good light, where you're comfortable talking about your health. Use headphones if other people are nearby.

## Your agreement

I have read this information and I agree to have this consultation by video, on these terms.
