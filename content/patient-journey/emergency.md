## Full

Carevia is not an emergency service. If you think you or someone with you needs urgent help, call 112 now or go to the nearest emergency department.

Don't wait for an online consultation, and don't use the waiting room to ask for urgent help. If things get worse before, during or after your consultation, call 112.
