title: We don't record your consultation
---

> Draft for review by Carevia's lawyer. Not final wording.

Sound and image from your consultation are not recorded, by Carevia or by your doctor.

Recording a consultation would need your separate, written consent, given on its own and not as part of these terms. Carevia doesn't ask for that consent, so recording is never switched on.

Carevia keeps only practical details, such as when your call started and ended, so we can help if the connection drops. What you and your doctor discuss is not kept by Carevia. Your doctor writes your clinical record in iMED, Carevia's clinical system.
