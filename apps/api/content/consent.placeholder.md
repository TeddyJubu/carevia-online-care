- Your consultation is a video call with a doctor registered with the Ordem dos Médicos.
- If your doctor decides you need an examination or urgent care, an in-person visit is the alternative.
- The price is shown before you pay.
- The call is not recorded.
- Carevia is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112.
